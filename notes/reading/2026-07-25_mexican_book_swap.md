# 2026-07-25 — The Mexican book swap, the bond desk, and the inverse sensitivity (read-log)

> Session: the fixture-book swap executed (`INT-21`, resolving `OQ-INT-04`) — 26 BMV equities + 30 counterparties incl. PEMEX/CFE debt-only, a new additive fixed-coupon bond desk (`CCR-RISK-02`), the Mexican-$a$ recompute $S_{\text{rate,eff}}^{MX} = 144{,}343{,}565$ MDP-2025 (−26.6%, `INT-22`), and the EE/PE band re-baselined on the book (mean EE shift −2,968 / −1,705 / −1,469 MXN, byte-identical re-runs).

1. **Brigo & Mercurio — *Interest Rate Models: Theory and Practice*, ch. 3 (one-factor short-rate models; the Hull–White bond-price reconstruction $P(t,T) = A(t,T)\,e^{-B(t,T)\,r_t}$ with $B(T)=(1-e^{-aT})/a$)** (`[BrigoMercurio2006]`): **why:** both new quantities of this session ride on this formula — the bond pricer (`CCR-RISK-02`) discounts every cebur cashflow through `SimulatedHW1FCurve`, and the $S_{\text{rate,eff}}$ inversion (`INT-22`) divides the target yield response by the loading $\bar B(T) = (1-e^{-aT})/(aT)$; without it, neither why the pricer needs no yield-curve data nor why a *larger* $a$ gives *larger* rate marks is understandable.
2. **Anyfantaki, Blix Grimaldi, Madeira, Malovaná & Papadopoulos — *Decoding climate-related risks in sovereign bond pricing*, Figure 8 and the EMDE-panel data section** (`[Anyfantaki2025]`): **why:** the source of the scenario slope $\beta = 4\times10^{-6}$ of 10Y yield per bn MDP behind $S_{\text{rate,eff}}$ — reading it makes clear the rate channel is an `INT-12` Path-B *scenario* (chart-read IRFs, EMDE panel), ~12× Mexico's own estimated upper bound from the `INT-18/19` pre-registered null, not a Mexican estimate.
3. **Glasserman — *Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering*, ch. 3 (generating sample paths: GBM exact simulation and lognormal log-return MLE)** (`[Glasserman2003]`): **why:** the 26 per-name GBM fits (`fit_gbm`, closed-form MLE, crisis windows excluded per `MKT-CALIB-03`) and the FIX-based `MXN_USD_FX_RATE` leg are all instances of this chapter's estimators; it also grounds why $\sigma$ from daily log-returns is the right flat proxy for the `[eng]` IV surfaces.
4. **Higham — *Computing the nearest correlation matrix — a problem from finance*** (`[Higham2002]`, §99): **why:** the PSD guard on the book's 28×28 pairwise-complete sample correlation (`INT-21`); the builder's eigenvalue-clip + renormalize is the cheap variant, and this paper is both its justification and the upgrade path (alternating projections) if the clip ever distorts structure.
5. **Cont & Tankov — *Financial Modelling with Jump Processes*, ch. 3** (`[ContTankov2004]`, re-read from the `INT-20` log): **why:** the compound rate $\lambda\,\mathbb{E}[\text{mark}]$ explains both the band ordering on the new book (headline > CT anchor > floor) and the `OQ-INT-11` caveat — a book-level mark applied uniformly to every name compounds to a ≈28%/yr expected drag per equity path.

Open decisions these readings feed: prospectus-verified bond terms and FRN/spread dynamics (`OQ-INT-10`), the sector-differentiation of the equity marks (`OQ-INT-11`), and the headline-metric choice on the real book (`OQ-INT-02`).
