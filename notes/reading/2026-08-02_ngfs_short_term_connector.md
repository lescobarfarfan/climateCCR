# Read-log — 2026-08-02 · The NGFS short-term connector and the first transition-channel results

Session decisions covered: `MKT-NGFS-04..07`, `INT-29`, `INT-30` (and the `MKT-NGFS-02/03` refinements). Readings ordered by priority: the first three are load-bearing for defending the build; the rest deepen specific legs.

1. **`[NGFS2025ST]` — NGFS, *Short-term Climate Scenarios — Technical Documentation V1.0* (May 2025).** Read: the four scenario narratives (the shock sequences per narrative), the model-coupling chapter (GEM-E3 → EIRIN → CLIMACRED, Steps 0–4), and the variable/coverage annex (who publishes what, at which grain). **Why:** this is the primary source for everything `MKT-NGFS-04..07` assumes — the WEO-Oct-2023 baseline anchoring (`MKT-NGFS-05`), EIRIN's 5-macro-region policy rate and CLIMACRED's country-grain sovereign adjustments (`MKT-NGFS-06`), and the "physical risk is predetermined over 3–5y" argument `INT-29` leans on. Without it the region-proxy caveat and the incl/excl-policy decomposition cannot be defended. The vault summary `[[ngfs_short_term_scenarios_summary]]` is the fast entry; the tech doc is the citable object.
2. **`[Vermeulen2021]` — Vermeulen et al., *The heat is on*, Ecological Economics 190:107205.** Read: the framework sections (scenario definition → risk-factor shocks; the interest-rate and equity shock construction) and the DNB 2018 Occasional Study's scenario tables. **Why:** the canonical peer-reviewed precedent for translating a transition scenario into *market risk-factor shocks* — the intellectual template for `MKT-NGFS-06`'s curve shock; the natural examiner question "who else maps scenarios to rate shocks?" lands here.
3. **`[FedCSA2024]` — Federal Reserve, *Pilot CSA Exercise* summary (May 2024).** Read: the transition-risk module design (scenario deltas applied to current balance sheets) and the participant-practice observations. **Why:** `MKT-NGFS-02/05` cite the Fed CSA translation practice; it is the supervisory precedent for "model-world delta, market-world landing point" — the exact structure of our $\Delta r$ on the current MXN curve.
4. **`[BoE2022CBES]` — Bank of England, *Results of the 2021 CBES* (May 2022) + the 2021 *Key elements* design note.** Read: the three-scenario design (Early/Late/No Additional Action) and the loss-drag readouts. **Why:** the HWTP+SWUC orderly/disorderly pairing (`MKT-NGFS-07`) mirrors the CBES span; the peak-adverse stress convention backs the signed-peak fixed flavor.
5. **`[Allen2020]` — Allen et al., Banque de France WP 774.** Read: the suite-of-models architecture (NGFS reference scenarios → domestic macro-financial variables) and the scenario-selection section. **Why:** the ACPR pilot is the template for a *country* build off NGFS scenarios — the closest structural analogue to landing NGFS deltas on Mexican instruments (`MKT-NGFS-05`).
6. **`[ECB2021EconomyWide]` — Alogoskoufis et al., ECB OP 281.** Read: the scenario-translation pillar (§ methodology) and the credit/market-risk conditioning. **Why:** promoted from §99 this session; backs the within-suite differencing of `MKT-NGFS-05` and (already) the `INT-24` sector disaggregation.
7. **`[Roncoroni2021]` — Roncoroni, Battiston, Escobar-Farfán & Martínez-Jaramillo, JFS 54:100870.** Read: the scenario design on the Mexican supervisory dataset and the network-valuation readout. **Why:** the closest published precedent for climate transition scenarios on *Mexican* financial institutions — the manuscript's Mexico-legitimacy anchor for `MKT-NGFS-04`.
8. **`[Riahi2017]` — Riahi et al., Global Environmental Change 42:153–168.** Read: §1–2 (the SSP framework and SSP2 "middle of the road"). **Why:** the only defensible NGFS↔SSP commonality — all NGFS long-term scenarios ride SSP2 socioeconomics — is the load-bearing sentence of the `INT-29` alignment note.
9. **`[NGFS2025Notes]` — NGFS, *2025 Explanatory notes on NGFS long-term scenarios* (Nov 2025).** Read: the physical-risk note (the damage-function retraction acknowledgement) and the key-assumptions note. **Why:** the official caveat `INT-29` converts into an argument *for* the HAZ-estimated physical channel; also the `[NGFS2024]` caveat's source.
10. **`[Gregory_xVA]` — Gregory, *The xVA Challenge* (4th ed.).** Read: the exposure chapters (exposure = $\max(V, 0)$, netting-set aggregation, EE/EPE). **Why:** the `INT-30` (a) non-monotonicity — the transition delta flipping sign with shock size — is a direct consequence of the exposure kink; this is the reference that turns the surprising number into a textbook mechanism.
11. **`[Huppmann2021]` — Huppmann et al., Open Research Europe 1:74.** Skim: the IIASA data-infrastructure description. **Why:** documents the access route `pipelines/15` replays (guest token, runs index, bulk timeseries); the tooling citation for the data chapter.
12. **`[BaudinoSvoronos2021]` — BIS FSI Insights 34** (skim) and **`[BanxicoUNEP2020]`** (skim the diagnosis chapters). **Why:** situate the design as supervisory mainstream and give the Mexican-system context paragraph its sources; `[BanxicoREF]` (§99) still needs its edition pinned (`OQ-MKT-13` d).
