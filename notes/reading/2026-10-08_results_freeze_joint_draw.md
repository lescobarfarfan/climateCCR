# Read-log — the results freeze, the per-path aggregation artefact and the joint book draw (2026-10-08)

Session decisions: `CCR-SIM-03` (joint simulation of the book as the `pipelines/01` default), `GEN-38` (the multi-seed Monte-Carlo precision study and the correction of the `GEN-30` tail caveat), the re-base annotations on `INT-23/25/28/32/34/35`, `CCR-RISK-06/08` and `GEN-34`, the three engine details logged as `OQ-CCR-11/12/13`, and the proposal structure guide `[[proposal_guide]]` (a vault note, no decision). Readings ordered by priority.

1. **`[Glasserman2003]` — Glasserman, *Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering*, §2.3.3 (sampling multivariate normals: every square root $A$ of the covariance with $AA^\top = C$ gives the same joint law, so Cholesky and SVD colourings are two valid realisations of one model) and §9.1 (the sample-quantile estimator and its variance $p(1-p)/\big(n f(q_p)^2\big)$).** Why: `GEN-38` measures exactly what §9.1 predicts — on 10,000 paths the 1y book q99 has a 0.4–0.6 % sd across master seeds — and §2.3.3 is why two valid draws cannot differ by the 10 % seen on 2026-10-03 unless something other than the draw changed: the comparison that exposed the aggregation artefact.
2. **`[Gregory_xVA]` ch. 7 (exposure: netting sets, the aggregation of exposure across counterparties, why a book-level quantile needs one scenario set) and `[PykhtinZhu2007]` §"Simulation framework" (scenario generation once, valuation per trade, aggregation per netting set).** Why: `CCR-SIM-03` restores the industry construct — one Monte-Carlo scenario set for the whole book — that PIMPA's per-portfolio session had replaced with an independent draw per counterparty; the per-NAID metrics were right all along and only cross-counterparty aggregates were ill-defined.
3. **`[BaselCRE]` CRE53 (Effective EPE is additive across netting sets; EAD per netting set) and CRE50 (netting set and margin period definitions).** Why: the reason the headline band, EEPE and CVA were unaffected by the artefact — they are sums of per-netting-set expectations — while the per-path book distribution of `GEN-34` was not; and why the joint draw changes those numbers only by a re-draw.
4. **`[ContTankov2004]` ch. 6 (simulating compound Poisson processes on a time grid; the law of the increments does not depend on the grid) and ch. 3 (independence of the jump and diffusion components).** Why: on the per-counterparty draw the Poisson counts followed each netting set's grid, so the book carried four independent climate-event streams; under the joint draw the events are binned once on the union grid and reach every counterparty through the model's own overlay (HW1F decay, GBM cumulative log-marks), a finer and law-preserving binning.
5. **`[Merton1976]` §2–3 (the jump-diffusion with jumps independent of the Brownian driver).** Why: the jump-off / jump-on pairing that the climate delta relies on — identical diffusion normals in both legs under one draw per leg — is the Merton independence assumption made operational; `test_joint_simulation` locks it on the fixture.
6. **`[Klugman2019]` ch. 9 (compound distributions: the mean of the aggregate is $\lambda E[L]$, invariant to how severity is split across labels when the per-label means are pinned).** Why: the merged-label variants of the S-tier sensitivity now re-fit per-label severity on merged groups with the `INT-26` mean-matching, so the sensitivity moves only the S matrix, not the expected impact.
7. **`[GolubVanLoan2013]` (§99) §4.2 and §8.6.** Why: the Cholesky factor is unique and the SVD basis is not — the two mixings that realised different cross-counterparty dependence on the per-counterparty draw; unchanged from the 2026-10-03 read-log, now with the measured consequence.
8. **`[eng]` the engine's own seam — `src/climateCCR/risk/ccr/evaluators/ccr_valuation_session.py` (`prepare` / `simulate` / `value`, `prepare_book`, `simulate_book`) and `src/climateCCR/simulation/multi_risk_factor_simulation.py` (`slice_scenarios`).** Why: the whole change is forty lines at two seams; reading them is faster than reading about them, and the fixture test `tests/risk_ccr/test_joint_simulation.py` shows every property the design claims.
