## The fix (commit `5607c5a`)

`price_single_trade` rewritten with corrected economics — per-period Act/365 accrual on every
cashflow, valuation-date-conditional cc forwards (both bonds functions of the same simulated
short rate), simulated-past fixings proxied by the valuation-date forward over the same span
(the engine stores no per-path fixing history — an architecture constraint, now documented),
and the spliced first period paying the **previous** fixing's historical rate with notional and
accrual. Conventions (#4, #5) documented in the docstring, unchanged.

**Goldens re-based** (deliberate `CCR-MIG-03` supersede, same commit): both fixture baselines
regenerated by their deterministic reconstructors under seed 233423 / n_paths 10000. Harness
materiality on the old fixture book: EE moved up to ~102 abs / PE ~297 abs (notional-100 trades);
the cc-vs-simple choice is second-order against that (~0.5% of the move). Full suite 280 passed.

## Consequence — live-chain re-base (2026-08-12/13 runs, this branch)

The Mexican book's IRS desk (30 × 100k MXN quarterly TIIE swaps) repriced ~÷4 in MtM magnitude,

## What the audit deliberately did not change

- The cc-vs-simple floating convention (#4) — a user decision; adopting simple Act/360 would align
  the IRS with `MKT-SIE-04`/the FRN leg at a ~1% PV effect and another goldens re-base. → **Resolved 2026-08-20 (`OQ-CCR-10` closed): simple Act/360 adopted, payer relabeled to market semantics, goldens + chain re-based — baseline BOOK EPE +0.024%, band −8.93/−5.44/−4.86% (second-decimal move, as the wedge predicted).**
- `CorrelationMatrix` internals modernization (#8) — deferred while the goldens hold the PSD
  branch's exact numerics; revisit if numpy removes `np.matrix`.
- The option pricer idiom (#10) — different component; noted only.
